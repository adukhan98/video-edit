## Canvas and safe zone (HARD RULE)

{{FORMATS}}

- Every piece of text, logo, icon and animated element stays inside the safe zone — and never
  animates through the unsafe margins (no fly-ins from off-canvas; enter with scale, fade or short
  slides that start inside the zone). Plain background fills (solid panels with no content) may run
  to the canvas edges.
- Slots render a **transparent, full-canvas** video at the output size and fps
  (HyperFrames `--format mov` → ProRes 4444 with alpha). Pixels you do not draw stay fully transparent.
- First and last frame of every slot are fully transparent: everything animates in and out inside
  the slot window.
- Minimum on-screen text: 30 px at 1080 wide (phone legibility). Labels 30–40 px, headlines 64–110 px.

## Motion

{{MOTION}}

Defaults when the brand is silent:
- GSAP, one paused timeline, seek-safe and deterministic (no unseeded randomness).
- Ease out for entrances (`power3.out`, `back.out(1.6)` for pops), `power2.inOut` for moves. Never linear.
- Entrances 0.25–0.45 s, exits 0.2–0.3 s. One new element at a time; hold the final state ≥ 1 s.
- Land each reveal ON the spoken word: start the tween ~0.15 s before the word's timestamp.
